FAQ

Fair questions.

What is a medication safety agent?+

An agent you brief in plain words. medcheck resolves your medicines to active ingredients, builds the interaction graph from FDA labels, scores mucosal risk, and writes a 24-hour schedule. Built for patients, caregivers and clinicians.

How is it different from asking ChatGPT?+

A chatbot starts from what you type. medcheck starts from official OpenFDA monographs and curated pharmacological rules, and every flag cites the label section it came from. No vibes, no hallucinated interactions.

Does it replace my doctor?+

No. It's informational clinical pharmacology, not a diagnosis. Nothing goes live without your approval, and the report is designed to be read with your prescribing physician, not instead of them.

Will it know Indian brand names?+

Yes — select international and Indian brand aliases (Crocin, Dolo, Ecosprin, Brufen, Omez…) resolve to generic active ingredients before any analysis runs.

Is my data stored or sold?+

Anonymous guest sessions. No signup, no card, no ads, no data selling. Your regimen stays in your session.

Is it really free?+

$0 forever, 0% of anything. The underlying label data is public. Safety shouldn't have a paywall.
